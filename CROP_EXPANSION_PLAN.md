# QÚNAR crop expansion plan

## Active ML crops
Wheat, corn, rice, cotton, sunflower, potato. These remain the only crops with the current district-year ML predictor.

## Statistical candidates for the next calibration
Barley, safflower, open-field vegetables, melons, grapes, pome and stone fruits, other fruits and berries, nuts.

The Bureau of National Statistics publishes updated crop area, gross harvest and yield series for Turkestan Region, including an agricultural historical workbook. The candidate crops must not receive ML predictions until a comparable district-year panel is assembled and validated.

Perennial crops (grapes, orchards, berries/nuts) should be calibrated separately because bearing area, age of plantations and multi-year investment horizons differ from annual field crops.

## Model rule
- ML tier: yield prediction + climate/resource/economic analysis.
- Statistical-screening tier: visible as a future calibration candidate, but excluded from the ML ranking until sufficient data are available.
