## Methods and sources
- FAO-56 Penman–Monteith ET0, chapters 2–4: https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e06.htm
- FAO crop Kc framework: https://www.fao.org/4/X0490E/x0490e0b.htm
- FAO empirical rainfall example: https://www.fao.org/4/S2022E/s2022e08.htm
- NASA POWER monthly API: https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/temporal/monthly/
- World Bank CCKP CMIP6: historical anomaly baseline 1995–2014, SSP2-4.5 and SSP5-8.5, 2040–2059.

NASA monthly query, when a user activates the option:
`T2M, T2M_MAX, T2M_MIN, RH2M, WS2M, PS, ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN, PRECTOTCORR`.
For precipitation NASA POWER monthly rate in mm/day is converted to
monthly totals using calendar days *before* averaging over 1995–2014.
Reference radiation is calculated for mid-month using the field latitude.
Surface pressure (PS) is used for the psychrometric constant and a pressure-
derived elevation approximation for clear-sky radiation.

FAO-56 ETo uses net shortwave/longwave radiation, the saturation vapor
pressure slope, surface pressure, wind, humidity and daily mean, max and min
temperatures. For May-Sep soil heat flux uses the monthly temperature gradient;
April G=0 because March is outside the six-month record. These are approximations
and are recorded as such.

Future Tmean, Tmin, Tmax shift by the CCKP monthly temperature anomalies,
future monthly precipitation by the monthly precipitation anomalies; all other
meteorological parameters are held fixed (unvalidated scenario assumption).
Future monthly precipitation cannot be negative.

ETc requires six user-supplied monthly Kc coefficients; the application does
not pretend to know field-specific crop planting/harvest dates. For optional
irrigation, effective rainfall uses the FAO training empirical monthly method
and user-selected irrigation application efficiency. Units: **1 mm = 10 m³/ha**, gross m³/ha = net mm × 10 / efficiency.

## Boundaries and nonclaims
- FAO-56 scenario is a *supplementary planning calculation*, not an official
  irrigation norm under Kazakhstan Order 108-NQ and not a measured water use.
- It does not modify the crop-optimizer water constraints automatically.
- It is not a crop growth model; no crop-yield losses or heat-stress physiology
  are inferred from ET0.
- It does not model crop Kc phenology, soil water storage, salinity, groundwater,
  conveyance losses, runoff physics, or additional ponding needs for rice.
- The NASA monthly historic point/polygon data and the former South Kazakhstan
  regional CCKP anomalies have a spatial scale mismatch; results are scenarios.
- If monthly POWER inputs are absent, the calculator stops without inventing
  numbers. User-specific coefficients are not replaced with unverified defaults.
- Historical ML training files, R², rankings and visible v0.8.1 label unchanged.

## Demo process
1. Pick a farm location or draw a polygon.
2. Choose Assess My Farm or Check & Select a Crop.
3. Choose CMIP6 SSP2-4.5 or SSP5-8.5 in Climate scenarios.
4. Open FAO-56 and opt in.
5. Run with no Kc: only ET0 is reported. Enter six Kc values and effective-rainfall
   assumption plus efficiency to obtain conditional ETc and water volume.
6. Check monthly table, source notes and export.
7. Verify baseline norms, hard water cap and primary optimizer unchanged.
