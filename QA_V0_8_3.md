# QŪNAR v0.8.3 - district-crop 2025 training update QA

## Crop machine learning
- Production Random Forest trains on every verified analytical-core row packaged in `data/analytical_panel.csv`.
- Training panel now covers 2004-2025.
- Final analytical sample: N=1362, 17 districts, 6 calibrated crops.
- 139 new district-crop observations were appended: 68 for 2024 and 71 for 2025.
- 2024-2025 outcomes come from the official district-level BNS crop-dynamics workbook; regional aggregates were not substituted.
- NASA POWER climate is joined at district-year level through 2025.
- Missing BNS markers remain missing and do not become zero-yield observations.

## Validation
- Primary GroupKFold-by-year: MAE=8.840 c/ha, RMSE=16.198 c/ha, R2=0.926.
- Forward block 2004-2010 -> 2011-2015: R2=0.949.
- Forward block 2004-2015 -> 2016-2020: R2=0.891.
- Forward block 2004-2020 -> 2021-2025: R2=0.808.
- Ten-year stress 2004-2013 -> 2014-2023: R2=0.850.
- Random Forest exceeds the median district-crop benchmark in every canonical forward block.
- Hyperparameter robustness grouped R2 range: 0.916-0.928.
